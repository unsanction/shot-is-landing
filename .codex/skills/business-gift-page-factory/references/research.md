# Business Research Checklist

Use this checklist before creating a gift page.

## Sources

Prioritize:
- official website and landing pages
- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn
- app store pages or product pages
- maps, menus, booking pages, marketplace listings
- press, interviews, founder posts, case studies
- recent customer reviews when tone or pain points matter

Avoid relying on one source unless the business has almost no public footprint.

## Extract

Write down:
- brand name and exact spelling
- location and operating area
- main products or services
- customer segments
- current content style
- recurring visual codes, colors, phrases, and formats
- near-term business hooks: seasonal launches, app, locations, events, hiring, franchise, B2B, retention
- credible proof points with source URLs

## Decide the angle

Pick one primary business goal:
- content scale
- app installs
- local/store traffic
- product launch
- franchise/partner leads
- hiring/recruiting
- ecommerce conversion
- retention/repeat purchase

The page should be about one primary goal. Secondary ideas can appear as variants.

## Copy Guardrails

- Personalize from observed facts.
- Do not overstate performance outcomes.
- Do not imply endorsement by the recipient.
- Do not claim the videos are approved by the recipient.
- Use “sample”, “preview”, “draft”, or “creative direction” until the business approves assets.
